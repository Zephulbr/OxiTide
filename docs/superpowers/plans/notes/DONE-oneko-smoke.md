# Oneko — manual smoke checklist

Requires a real account and a real window; record date and result per item.

- [ ] Settings → Appearance shows the Cat checkbox, reduced-motion note appears
      only when the OS animation setting is off, and both credits links open GitHub.
- [ ] Enable Cat: the cat appears (initial position restored or default), walks
      10px/tick toward the cursor, and stops ~48px away.
- [ ] Idle: after a pause she falls asleep in place (~20s roll); edge scratching
      happens when idling near a window edge.
- [ ] Drag: left-drag moves her; she scratches against the drag direction; release
      and she resumes chasing.
- [ ] Sleep perch, wave mode (Atmosphere): double-click sends her to the 60% point
      of the seek wave; she tires, curls up, and stays put.
- [ ] Sleep perch, line mode (Instrument): same on the flat seek line.
- [ ] Sleep perch, mini player: same on the inline seek bar.
- [ ] Wake: double-click while asleep resumes chasing.
- [ ] Nothing playing: double-click sleeps her in place.
- [ ] Track ends while asleep: she settles where she is instead of drifting.
- [ ] Resize the window while awake and asleep: she stays in bounds; while asleep
      she moves to the new perch position.
- [ ] Toggle Cat off: she disappears instantly; toggle on: she returns.
- [ ] Restart the app: flag and position are restored; she starts awake.
- [ ] Reduced motion on: no cat appears; note explains why.
- [ ] Click-through: click a button she has walked near; clicks land normally.
