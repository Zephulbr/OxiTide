## How the drafts were made

- One agent per language worked from the English catalogs, translator notes, the glossary and
  a shared brief (ICU rules, plural categories, protected terms, register), wrote drafts
  outside the repo and had to pass a validator (`compareMessages`, as `check.mjs`) before
  stopping. Drafts went in through `apply-draft.mjs`; no message was rejected.
- Models: Claude Opus 5.5 for the first 16 languages; the account rate limit then cut runs
  short, so a 60-message trial (the hardest message kinds, into Ukrainian) compared Haiku 5.5
  and Sonnet 5.5. Haiku declined inserted names despite the rule, skipped the `=1` rule and
  made a few meaning errors; Sonnet made none, so Sonnet finished uk, tr, id, pt-BR, ko,
  zh-Hans and zh-Hant and the follow-up redraft.
- Chinese was meant to be drafted by DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and cross-checked by Claude
  (I18N-D8). OpenCode Go's weekly limit was reached and the free tier needs a training-data
  opt-in, so Claude drafted both Chinese variants and they ship as `machine`, not
  `cross-checked`. A DeepSeek review pass can be added later (`--status cross-checked`).
- Fixed by hand before loading: Indonesian evening greeting ("sore" → "malam"; the brief now
  states the hour ranges).

## English changed during drafting (agents' findings)

- `remoteSettings.firewallUnknown`: the "cannot read its rules" sentence came after "run this
  once in a terminal:"; it now comes first.
- `remoteSettings.autoAddressDefault` (new): the remote's network address no longer reuses
  `device.icon.auto` (gendered languages need different forms).
- `playlist.keptDuplicates` / `addedCopiesKept`: "Kept one of the duplicate" → "Skipped the
  duplicate" / "…, skipped # duplicates".
- The Profiles window title uses `nav.profiles`; `route.profiles` ("Artists") is the artist
  filter/shelf only (notes updated). Better notes for `device.fromPlayback`, `device.icon.auto`.
- These four messages were redrafted in all 23 languages in one pass.

  from memory; TIDAL's own terms for Save for Later, Made for You, My Mixes, Jump back in and
  Collaborators were guesses in most languages; "Library" was rendered as "collection" in most
  (matching My Collection), "Bibliothek/Biblioteca" in a few (uk mixes both).
- `de` du; revoke "Entkoppeln"; library "Sammlung". `fr` vous; "Bypass" kept. `es` tú;
  Rack jargon "Pletina". `it` tu. `pt-BR` você; `=0` forms added (CLDR treats 0 as `one`);
  `later.removed` gender guessed. `nl` je; "keer afgespeeld" for plays is awkward. `sv`, `nb`,
  `da` du; "Förlustfri" vs the English tier name. `fi` sinä. `pl` ty; `history.daysAgo` made
  plural. `cs` vy (formal). `hu` te. `uk` ти; credits "Автори". `ru` вы; units kept Latin
  (kHz, dB) to match code-formatted values. `ro` tu. `el` εσύ (questions below). `tr` sen;
  queue "Sıra" (TIDAL may say "Kuyruk"). `id` kamu. `ja` です/ます. `ko` 합니다; explicit badge
