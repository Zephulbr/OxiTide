## What ships

- Queue: "Add to queue" lands after the user-added tracks still to play, else right after the
  current track, so added tracks play before the rest of the context, in order (`Queue::push`,
  `Queue::added_end`; `docs/agents/audio.md`). Play next joins that run; shuffle keeps it next and
  in order; Autoplay appends after it. The queue rail's drop below the last row inserts at the
  end explicitly (`api.queueInsert`). Desktop and phone alike.
- Phone remote: a filter box on Playlist pages and Library → Tracks (title or artist, whole list;
  a played row queues the matches, then the rest); Play next leads every track menu (the already
  allowlisted `queue_play_next`). Six new `remote.json` strings, translations copied from the
  desktop's `app.json` (machine status). No command, config or TIDAL-client change.
