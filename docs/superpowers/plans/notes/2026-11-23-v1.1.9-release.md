## What ships

Changes since `v1.1.8`:

- `34cc04de` fix(paging): `get_all_pages` logs each walk when it ends (items, windows, empty
  windows, TIDAL's total, ms) and a long one's total before it starts; path ids masked
  (`users/*/favorites/artists`).
- `ed9643af` fix(paging): a walk with a known total stops after 10 empty windows in a row
  (1,000 rows at 100 a window), with a WARN; offsets are made a group at a time. Issue #4's
  read-past-empty-windows rule holds below that.
- `093225d8` fix(ui): Profiles shows the own card while the favorites load or after they fail;
  `viewCache` shares a running fetch for `JOIN_MS` (60 s) only.
- No IPC, config, persisted-state or locale-catalog change.
