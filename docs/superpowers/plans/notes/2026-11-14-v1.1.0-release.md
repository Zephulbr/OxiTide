## What ships

- The UI, the phone remote and the tray/taskbar text in English plus 23 machine-drafted
  languages; language setting and picker; locale-aware formatting and sorting; fonts for
  Latin Extended, Cyrillic, Greek and CJK; translated engine notices and error headlines.
- Accessibility: Windows contrast themes (A-3), track-change announcements (A-4), interface
  zoom (A-5). A-1 (`lang`) and A-2 (translated accessible names) came with the extraction.
- Volunteer workflow: `CONTRIBUTING.md` ("Translating"), the translation issue template, the
  `scripts/i18n/` tools; `check.mjs` runs in CI.
- One new command, `set_zoom` (sync, `NOT_EXPOSED`), and config keys `[ui] language`, `[ui]
  zoom`. No audio, output or TIDAL-client change.
