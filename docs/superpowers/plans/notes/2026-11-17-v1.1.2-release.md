## What ships

- Shuffle: turning it on shuffles the whole queue (played tracks too); a new play with shuffle
  on plays the pick first and shuffles the rest; queue jumps keep the order.
- Collection Tracks with a filter: matches, then the rest of the collection.
- Search: a song plays first, then its artist's other tracks (first page at once, the rest via
  the new `queue_extend_context`); an artist Top result plays all their tracks.
- Compact track lists show the now-playing mark on the cover.
- New command `queue_extend_context` (sync, not exposed to the phone remote); `play_tracks`
  now returns the queue revision. No config, output, DSP or TIDAL-client change.
