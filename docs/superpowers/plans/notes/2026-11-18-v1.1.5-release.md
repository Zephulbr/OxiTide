## What ships

- Phone remote: the filter box (`remote/components/FilterBox.svelte`) on every list with 20 or
  more items (`FILTER_MIN`); shorter lists show none. Track lists: Mix, Album and Recently played
  join Playlist and Tracks (title or artist, whole list, matches then the rest; album rows keep
  their numbers). Library grids (`TileGrid.svelte`): Albums (title, artists, year), Artists
  (name), My Mixes (title, description). Playlists: names in the open folder. Five new
  `remote.json` strings, machine drafts in every shipped language. UI only: no Rust, command,
  config or TIDAL-client change.
