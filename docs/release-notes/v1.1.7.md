OxiTide 1.1.7 starts a song as soon as you click it in a long list, marks the playing song in the phone's queue, and lets list filters ignore punctuation and accents. Everything else is the same as in 1.1.6.

> OxiTide is an unofficial, third-party project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to TIDAL. A paid TIDAL subscription is required. OxiTide plays only what your own account can already stream.

## Fixes

- **A song you click in a long list plays at once.** The first time you played something from your Tracks after opening the app, OxiTide downloaded the whole list first, and no other song could be clicked until it was done ([#7](https://github.com/Zephulbr/OxiTide/issues/7)). Now the song starts right away and the rest of the list joins the queue as it loads, in Tracks, playlists and an artist's All tracks. With shuffle on, the rest is still shuffled in with the whole list. The Shuffle buttons, and lists you have filtered or sorted, still wait for the whole list so they can play from all of it, but the list stays clickable while they wait. A song you add to the queue during the load no longer cuts the queue short, and if the load fails, Retry adds the rest.

## Improvements

- **The phone's queue shows where you are.** The playing song stands out (a highlight, an accent bar and a turning disc over its cover), songs already played are faded, and the queue opens on the playing song instead of at the top. It follows along to the next song while that row is on screen.
- **List filters ignore punctuation and accents**, on the desktop and the phone. "ac dc" or "acdc" finds AC/DC, "beyonce" finds Beyoncé, and "guns n roses" finds Guns N' Roses. TIDAL's own search is unchanged.

## Install

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (x64) | `OxiTide_1.1.7_x64-setup.exe` (per user, recommended) or the `.msi` (per machine) |
| Linux (x86_64) | `.AppImage` or `.deb`; `PKGBUILD` for Arch (AUR recipe) |

Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. An installed 0.3 or later copy offers the update in **Settings → System → Updates**. deb and AUR installs update through the package manager.

## Known limits and Linux

Unchanged from 1.1: Windows builds are not code-signed (SmartScreen: **More info → Run anyway** after checking the hash), the phone remote uses plain HTTP on your local network, and Linux is a work in progress with the known issues listed in the [1.0 release notes](https://github.com/Zephulbr/OxiTide/releases/tag/v1.0.0).
