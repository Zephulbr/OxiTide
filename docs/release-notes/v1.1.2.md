OxiTide 1.1.2 fixes shuffle and makes plays from search and from a filtered Tracks list carry on the way TIDAL's own app does ([#3](https://github.com/Zephulbr/OxiTide/issues/3)). Output, audio quality and everything else are the same as in 1.1.

> OxiTide is an unofficial, third-party project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to TIDAL. A paid TIDAL subscription is required. OxiTide plays only what your own account can already stream.

## Changes

- **Shuffle takes in the whole queue.** Turning shuffle on while playing from the middle of a list now shuffles every track, including the ones before the current song. The current song keeps playing and moves to the top of the queue.
- **Shuffle stays on for new plays.** With shuffle on, playing a song from an album, playlist or list plays that song first and shuffles the rest. Before, the button stayed lit but the new queue played in order. Clicking a song in the queue itself still keeps the queue's order.
- **Search plays the artist.** Clicking a song in Explore's results, or a song shown as the Top result, plays it and then the rest of that artist's tracks (their page's Tracks tab), instead of handing over to Autoplay. An artist shown as the Top result plays all their tracks. The song starts right away; the rest of a large catalogue joins the queue a moment later.
- **Filtered Tracks carry on.** In **My Collection → Tracks**, playing a song while the filter is set queues the matching songs first, then the rest of your collection.
- **Now playing in search.** Explore's track list marks the playing song on its cover.

## Install

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (x64) | `OxiTide_1.1.2_x64-setup.exe` (per user, recommended) or the `.msi` (per machine) |
| Linux (x86_64) | `.AppImage` or `.deb`; `PKGBUILD` for Arch (AUR recipe) |

Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. An installed 0.3 or later copy offers the update in **Settings → System → Updates**. deb and AUR installs update through the package manager.

## Known limits and Linux

Unchanged from 1.1: Windows builds are not code-signed (SmartScreen: **More info → Run anyway** after checking the hash), the phone remote uses plain HTTP on your local network, and Linux is a work in progress with the known issues listed in the [1.0 release notes](https://github.com/Zephulbr/OxiTide/releases/tag/v1.0.0).
