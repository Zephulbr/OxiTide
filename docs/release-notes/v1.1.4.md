OxiTide 1.1.4 makes songs you add to the queue play before the rest of the playlist, and gives the phone remote a filter for your playlists and Tracks plus a Play next button. Everything else is the same as in 1.1.3.

> OxiTide is an unofficial, third-party project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to TIDAL. A paid TIDAL subscription is required. OxiTide plays only what your own account can already stream.

## Changes

- **Added songs play first.** **Add to queue** now puts songs after the ones you added earlier, or right after the current song if there are none, instead of at the end of the playlist or album you're playing. Your added songs play next, in the order you added them, and then the playlist carries on. **Play next** still puts a song right after the current one. Turning shuffle on leaves your added songs next and in order, and Autoplay's suggestions always come after them. To put songs at the very end, drag them onto the queue below its last row.
- **Filter on the phone remote.** Playlist pages and **Library → Tracks** on the phone have a filter box that finds songs by title or artist across the whole list. Tapping a match plays it, followed by the other matches and then the rest of the list.
- **Play next on the phone remote.** Every song's menu on the phone now starts with **Play next**.

## Install

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (x64) | `OxiTide_1.1.4_x64-setup.exe` (per user, recommended) or the `.msi` (per machine) |
| Linux (x86_64) | `.AppImage` or `.deb`; `PKGBUILD` for Arch (AUR recipe) |

Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. An installed 0.3 or later copy offers the update in **Settings → System → Updates**. deb and AUR installs update through the package manager.

## Known limits and Linux

Unchanged from 1.1: Windows builds are not code-signed (SmartScreen: **More info → Run anyway** after checking the hash), the phone remote uses plain HTTP on your local network, and Linux is a work in progress with the known issues listed in the [1.0 release notes](https://github.com/Zephulbr/OxiTide/releases/tag/v1.0.0).
