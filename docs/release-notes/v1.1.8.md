OxiTide 1.1.8 puts your playlist back in order when you turn shuffle off. Everything else is the same as in 1.1.7.

> OxiTide is an unofficial, third-party project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to TIDAL. A paid TIDAL subscription is required. OxiTide plays only what your own account can already stream.

## Fixes

- **Turning shuffle off restores the original order.** Until now the queue stayed shuffled after you turned shuffle off, on the desktop and the phone ([#7](https://github.com/Zephulbr/OxiTide/issues/7)). Now the playlist goes back to its order, the song playing keeps playing, and the next song is the one after it in the playlist. Songs you added to the queue still play next, in the order you added them, and Autoplay's suggestions stay at the end. This also works after a restart, for queues started in 1.1.8 or later; a queue saved by an older version keeps its order when you turn shuffle off.

## Install

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (x64) | `OxiTide_1.1.8_x64-setup.exe` (per user, recommended) or the `.msi` (per machine) |
| Linux (x86_64) | `.AppImage` or `.deb`; `PKGBUILD` for Arch (AUR recipe) |

Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. An installed 0.3 or later copy offers the update in **Settings → System → Updates**. deb and AUR installs update through the package manager.

## Known limits and Linux

Unchanged from 1.1: Windows builds are not code-signed (SmartScreen: **More info → Run anyway** after checking the hash), the phone remote uses plain HTTP on your local network, and Linux is a work in progress with the known issues listed in the [1.0 release notes](https://github.com/Zephulbr/OxiTide/releases/tag/v1.0.0).
