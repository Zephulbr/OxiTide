The Small Things

Orla Venn LyricsNextInfo

  1. Each track loads ahead at full speed, so a short drop goes unheard.

  2. From LRCLIB, fetched when you open them. Click a line to jump there.

  4. Play, pause and skip from any app, on keys you choose.
